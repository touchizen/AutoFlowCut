A YhhmEf send that arrives after the deadline has nothing to bind to: `routeRpcSend` skips completed gens (:93), and after the delete its loadend is dropped as `unbound` (:137). If the server charges after that single re-read, the result stays `flow-submit-not-sent` + `postClick`. The hook writes no ids, and the kind text tells the user to try again. — Scenario: the page's reCAPTCHA execute plus the batchexecute send take more than 15 s after the trusted click returns: (a) a modal is open, so `withAutomationViewport`'s finally puts the view back to 0×0 right after the click and the hidden page is throttled; (b) a slow network. The server then charges 10 credits and the response is discarded. The row is fresh with null ids, so the next Start, or the retry the message suggests, charges another 10 credits for the same scene. — Fix: stop treating the send deadline as final for YhhmEf. If that is too much, at least do a second nzlxg re-read after a grace period longer than the observed ~5.7 s YhhmEf latency before returning not-sent, and escalate to `flow-submit-lost` if credits dropped. Also log/report an unbound YhhmEf loadend that parses to a UUID (`media=<8>`) so the charged media can be found. Pin: a send at 16 s after the click must not end as `flow-submit-not-sent`.
