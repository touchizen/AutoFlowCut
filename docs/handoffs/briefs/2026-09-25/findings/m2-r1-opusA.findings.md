4. [MINOR] electron/ipc/flow-angular.js:446-456 and 324-331 — The not-sent vs lost split is timed from arm, not from the click. Separately, `generate-button-click-failed` is returned without `postClick`, and the gen is deleted, even when trustedClickLocked fails after mouseDown/mouseUp were sent (shared.js:320-327, "View collapsed mid-click"). — Scenario: a slow click or late send goes out after the deadline. Re-read credits after a grace period. Have the click helper report `dispatched`, and treat a dispatched failure as `postClick` while keeping the gen armed.
